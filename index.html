Of the Waters

Four centuries after the Collapse, the jump drive has stitched the old colonies back together. Between the bright stars the lines run fast. Out among the red dwarfs, the campfires are few and far between, and the dark between them goes on forever.

Human colonies are tiny lighted bubbles adrift in a vast ocean of empty darkness. Of the waters, we know nothing.
