Orion's Arm

Much of the science behind the starmap and the planetary profiles draws on the Orion's Arm Universe Project, used with the project's permission. That includes the world classification schemes (NoLWoCS and EWoCS), and ideas about alien biochemistry, biomes, ecology and exotic, non-chemical life. We use their terminology and ideas throughout, always in our own words. This one credit covers every use on the site. Orion's Arm and its content belong to the Orion's Arm Universe Project and its contributors.

Stars and planets

  • Star positions, distances and spectra: the HYG database, compiled from Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese catalogue data, with Gaia astrometry for nearby stars.
  • Known exoplanets: the Open Exoplanet Catalogue, including data it draws from the NASA Exoplanet Archive.
  • Everything else in a planetary system is generated, and the campaign's names, routes and history are invented.
