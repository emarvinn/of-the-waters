Ships, stations, wrecks, probes and anomalies. A moving object starts at a known system or dwarf and flies toward another, or toward a set of coordinates. Its marker sits where it would be in the campaign year, using the same rocket model as the sublight calculator. An object and its trajectory stay off the map until its launch year. Sublight courses bow slightly: navigators trim against the galactic magnetic field and swing wide of unmapped substellar bodies, which are deadly at a fraction of light speed.

Constant thrust, flip at the midpoint to stop. The fuel limit (fuel mass as a multiple of dry mass) sets peak speed: once the tanks hold only enough to brake, the ship coasts. Leave it blank for unlimited fuel. Results show in the readout for any selected system or plotted course.

Reading the map

Positions are true 3D, in light-years, galactic frame: Sol at the centre, the disc of rings is the galactic plane, +X points to the galactic core. Drop lines show height above or below the plane. Sphere size and colour give spectral class; extra spheres are companion stars.
